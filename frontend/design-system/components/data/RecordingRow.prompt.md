The dense alternative to the card grid, for libraries with hundreds of recordings.

```jsx
<RecordingRow name="The house on Carrer Nou" duration="48:12" state="done" peaks={rec.peaks} selected />
```

36px is the floor — do not compress it further. State is a glyph here, not a chip: there is no room for the word. The 20px waveform is exactly why rounded bars won over a filled envelope.
