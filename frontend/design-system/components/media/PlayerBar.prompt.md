The persistent player, pinned to the bottom of the shell inside the 12px panel gap. It survives navigation — playback never stops because the user moved to another view.

```jsx
<PlayerBar title="Field recording, long take" library="Field recordings" played={0.375} position="18:04" duration="48:12" />
```

Only one exists per session. The waveform here always shows the playhead. Pass no `peaks` and the slot collapses to the position and the total — which is what the bar shows while the recording being played is the one on screen, and for one whose peaks job has not run. Pass `onSeek` to make the waveform seekable, which §3.1 says it is: it is the widest target in the player, the bar already keeps clicks on it out of `onOpen`, and without it the strip takes a pointer and does nothing with it. Take `onPreview` alongside it and feed the previewed moment back through `position`, so the number beside the drawing says where a drag is going rather than where the sound still is. Pass `onSpeed` to make the rate pill a control rather than a readout; without it the pill still keeps its clicks out of `onOpen`, because a click on the speed is never a request to go somewhere. Pass `onClose` to draw the control that stops playback and dismisses the bar, and `href` with `onOpen` to make the bar a way into the recording it is playing — the title becomes the link, and the space around the controls opens it too.
